Idol of Yunshul

"The Idol of Yun'shul is a ritual idol and Relic connected to the Drowned God Yun'Shul, that allows its user to redeem one wish from anywhere and without the need of a Resonance. (one-time use, re-obtainable) Note: Using the Idol of Yun'Shul to escape The Depths can only be done once per character." You will only get 3 kinds of wishes, Escape the Depths,Re-roll your Bell, and to remove the flaw you started with.
